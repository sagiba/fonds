# Code of conduct

Fonds is a small project, and we want it to stay a good place to work together.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem obvious.
- Keep discussion on topic and constructive.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information, including financial data, without their
  consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may
restrict or block participation, when this policy is not followed.

The project does not yet have a private channel for conduct reports. Until it
does, report abuse to GitHub through its
[reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Security vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
